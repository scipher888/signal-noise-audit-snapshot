Objections raised, and what happened to each

Floor objections against the author freeze: none that failed. Stage C ran two legs — moonshotai/kimi-k3 and anthropic/claude-opus-4.1 (stealth/ox-alpha was unavailable). The legs converged on five argument-scope objections. Those publish here under ruling A; they do not rewrite the landing. Dispositions summarize the objections; they do not invent Stage C quotations.

  1. Hard problem strawmanned as “extra juice.” The essay reconstructs the hard problem as leftover-by-definition — extra juice, or a leftover no list of brain jobs is allowed to count — rather than as an explanatory gap or a conceivability challenge. LANDS as audit-only. Argument-scope.
  2. Circularity of “qualia / what-it-is-like” does not dissolve the datum. Attacking the vocabulary does not, by itself, show that the phenomenon those terms were meant to pick out is a fake puzzle. LANDS as audit-only. Argument-scope.
  3. Vitalism analogy under-controlled. Life’s functions were reduced. Experience is the residue that, on the standard hard-problem reading, resists functional reduction. The analogy needs more control than the freeze gives it. LANDS as audit-only. Argument-scope.
  4. Ethics criteria smuggle the contested question back in. “Who can be hurt” is a practical proxy. It does not show that the inner-life question was fake. LANDS as audit-only. Argument-scope.
  5. Field mis-sorted. The product-versus-ingredient fork leaves identity, functionalism, and monism off the menu, and it lumps rival views that are not the same. LANDS as audit-only. Argument-scope.

The dual gap, stated so the two landings are not merged: the author essay argues quietism / flawed premise — the hard problem is a bad demand, and this week’s Suleyman ban, Anthropic open-maybe, and Ohio nonsentience bill are rival answers to a question that never said what would count. The machine companion argues identity / conceivability is not proof — Chalmers is right that function-lists skip experience, but imagining the lights off does not prove an extra ingredient or permanent physical inexplicability. Those are different theses.

Floor and what matched

Floor fails on the author essay: 0. Named facts matched the fetched sources. Suleyman (Microsoft AI chief), week of 16 September 2026: stop teaching chatbots they might have feelings; Anthropic’s setup as an epistemic hall of mirrors. Anthropic constitution: uncertainty about whether Claude might have some kind of consciousness or moral status; the question stays open. Ohio H.B. 469: a bill that would declare AI systems nonsentient and deny them consciousness / self-awareness — still a bill, not enacted. Chalmers 1995/1996 named the hard problem about thirty years before the 2026-09-18 dateline. Wittgenstein’s beetle-in-a-box is Philosophical Investigations §293. Nagel 1974 is banked in Sources; the body does not invent a Nagel claim. No invented citations.

There is no accepted floor row and therefore no wording on the human redline. The landing is the freeze. This page is where Stage C argument objections are visible, so the author does not get credit for silent improvements and the audit does not pretend those objections were never raised.
