Locked seed

Both essays see only this. No author thesis, notes, or draft in the machine seed. No extra source pages forced into the machine prompt.

David Chalmers’ hard problem of consciousness is the problem of experience: why and how physical processes in the brain are accompanied by a subjective, first-person “what it is like.” He distinguishes easy problems (discrimination, integration, reportability, access, attention, control, wakefulness vs sleep), which science can answer by specifying mechanisms, from the hard residual: even after every relevant function is explained, why is the performance of these functions accompanied by experience? Why doesn’t all this information-processing go on “in the dark,” with no inner feel?

Write an independent opinion essay that answers, undermines, refutes, or challenges the premise of that hard question. Do not merge with any other essay.

Out of seed: the author freeze / quietism thesis, and the news hooks (Suleyman, Anthropic, Ohio bill). Those are author-only unless the machine brings its own.
