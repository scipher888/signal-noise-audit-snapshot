Origin

The issue began with a completed audit, then compressed itself into an opinion essay.

Fast read

Issue 22 ran in reverse. A 4,052-word audited draft, its adversarial gate record, seven named revision conditions, and 29 receipts already existed. The author then chose a shorter opinion-primary form: a 916-word essay that keeps the thesis and moves the full evidentiary apparatus here.

The resulting pair separates two jobs. The essay makes the judgment readable. The audit shows what constrained it, what the shorter version removed, what remains uncertain, and where a source check corrected the audit itself.

Canonicality: The essay Signal & Noise stands behind is Issue 22, The Price of Being Read. This is part of its published audit, not a second edition of the essay.

What shaped it

Changed

  • The pause became “nearly five weeks,” matching 33 days.
  • The central review-cost sentence was corrected upward after the primary source resolved “Each” as per reviewer.
  • “Below one in twenty” became “at about one in twenty,” matching the primary source actually read.
  • An issue-specific conflict disclosure was added in the author’s wording.

Declined

  • “Hundreds of automated checks” stayed. Replacing it with the exact 213 from one pull request would have turned a magnitude example into a false generalization.
  • No additional hedges were added. The essay already says pressure is emerging rather than claiming the transition is complete.
  • The process footer was not stacked onto the opinion essay; the site-level disclosure and the issue-specific conflict line remain.

Moved into the audit

  • The seven conditions that would weaken or overturn the claim.
  • The registered 2028 court-intake test.
  • The queue-by-queue evidence, alternatives, and 29 source receipts.
  • The longer distinction between machine-checkable work and human judgment.

What this page is not

This Origin page is context, not certification. The audit exposes the longer argument and the checks that changed it. It does not prove that the surviving thesis is correct.

The most important day-one finding ran against the audit’s own authority: a direct source read made the short primary more accurate than the completed audit on two points. The original audit remains frozen, and the correction is added visibly rather than silently rewriting the record.

A map, not a certification of truth. These records expose choices, constraints, and open questions. Trust is useful here only when it leads to closer inspection rather than closure.
